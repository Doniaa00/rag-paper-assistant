# Background Reading

Papers referenced for context during corpus curation but deliberately excluded
from the indexed RAG corpus (`data/shortlist_papers.csv`). Kept here so the
exclusion decision and rationale aren't lost.

## He, H. & Garcia, E. A. (2009)

**"Learning from Imbalanced Data."** IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data
Engineering, 21(9), 1263–1284. DOI: [10.1109/TKDE.2008.239](https://doi.org/10.1109/TKDE.2008.239)

Foundational survey on imbalanced learning, referenced for context but not
included in the indexed RAG corpus — no legitimate open-access copy available
(IEEE Xplore paywalled).
